Prebuilt patched Rust compiler with LLVM and integrated LLD, plus its matching
wasm32-wasip1 standard library. Locally recompressed as gzip, split into 8 MiB
files for static hosting. SHA-256 integrity metadata is in toolchain/manifest.json.
This is a pinned experimental compiler distribution, not current stable Rust.
Rust: MIT OR Apache-2.0. LLVM and LLD: Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception.
